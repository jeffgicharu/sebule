Most of what you see on this site is on our showroom floor, in more than one colour, and every fabric and wood finish is there to touch.

## What to expect

- Somebody who knows how each piece is made, and no pressure to buy.
- Fabric and finish samples to take home.
- Help with measuring up. Bring the size of your room, or a photo of it.

## Collecting an order

Orders placed for collection are collected here, free of charge. We write to you when yours is ready. Bring your order number, and a vehicle large enough for what you bought. We will help you load it.
