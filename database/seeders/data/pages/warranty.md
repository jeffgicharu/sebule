Our furniture is built to be used every day, and it is guaranteed.

| What | Guaranteed for |
|---|---|
| Solid wood tables, beds, storage and frames | 3 years |
| Sofas and upholstered chairs | 2 years on the frame, 1 year on fabric and cushions |
| Lighting | 1 year |
| Baskets, cushions and decor | 6 months |

Each product page gives the guarantee for that piece.

## What is covered

Faults in the materials and in how the piece was made: joints that work loose, frames that crack, drawers that stop running, stitching that fails.

## What is not covered

- Ordinary wear, and marks from use.
- Damage from water, direct sun, pests or an accident.
- Wood moving a little with the seasons. Solid wood does this, and it is not a fault.
- A piece that somebody else has altered or repaired.

## Making a claim

[Contact us](/contact) with your order number and a photo of the fault. We repair where we can and replace where we cannot.
